Message review

Employee disclosure. When this is on, employees who join with your company code are shown a notice and must accept it before joining. After that, the original and polished text of each message they replace is stored (encrypted, auto-deleted after 30 days) and visible to company admins here. Drafts that are only previewed are never stored. Every time an admin opens an original it is recorded in the audit log below. Check your local privacy and employment rules before enabling.
